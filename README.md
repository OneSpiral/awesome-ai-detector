# Awesome AI Detector

A curated, maintained list of AI-text detectors and open detection benchmarks,
with honest notes: detection is probabilistic, not proof, and no tool can
guarantee results against every future model.

> Selection criteria: a tool must be currently maintained, state its pricing or
> free tier, and publish a privacy policy. Entries note limits and unknowns; no
> entry is sponsored.

## Free detectors

- **EssayDetector** – free per-sentence AI detector with per-sentence
  confidence; no signup. States the probabilistic caveat on the page.
  https://essaydetector.org
- **HumanBench** – free detector plus an open, weekly-updated benchmark of
  detectors, models and humanizers. https://humanbench.org
- **GPTZero** – free tier with per-sentence highlighting; paid plans for
  educators and teams. https://gptzero.me
- **Copyleaks AI Content Detector** – free tier; paid API and enterprise.
  https://copyleaks.com
- **Smodin AI Detector** – free tier with credits. https://smodin.io
- **ZeroGPT** – free tier with daily limits. https://zerogpt.com
- **Winston AI** – free trial; paid plans. https://gowinston.ai
- **Content at Scale AI Detector** – free with limits. https://contentatscale.ai
- **Coda One** – Free AI detector with no signup; also offers a rewriter and grammar checker. https://www.codaone.ai/

## Paid / API detectors

- **Originality.ai** – paid detector + plagiarism with API; known for
  strictness. https://originality.ai
- **GPTZero API** – paid API access for platforms. https://gptzero.me
- **Turnitin** – institutional academic detection (not consumer-direct).
  https://turnitin.com

## Open benchmarks

- **HumanBench** – open, weekly-updated detector/model/humanizer rankings with
  methodology and dates. https://humanbench.org
- **AI Text Classifier evals** – see the leaderboard methodology on HumanBench
  and each listed tool's own published accuracy notes.

## Honest-use notes

- Detectors are probabilistic signals, not proof of authorship. False positives
  and negatives exist, and results vary by model and language.
- Never base an accusation, grade or hiring decision on a single detector
  output.
- Review each tool's privacy policy before submitting text that contains
  personal or unpublished work.
